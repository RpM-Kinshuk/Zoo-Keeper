## The five corrections

1. **Real-model truth from documented cases (D8).** A real case counts when
   whoever made it published how: a vendor's own quantized or FP8 release, a
   merge that ships its recipe file, official training stages, or a refusal
   removal made with a public tool whose settings are recorded. No contact
   with model makers is needed. Cases with only a declared relation stay
   descriptive and never block work.
2. **Score the 4 Heretic models (track B, by Nov 12).**
   - Use a per-matrix rule: the edited writer matrices change from the parent
     at low rank, within the recipe's rank. Write the pass rule first.
   - Controls (SFT, DPO and LoRA children, and unedited models) must not
     pass.
   - This makes the refusal-removal check realistic.
3. **Redesign scaling (D9).**
   - Pools of all public models sharing the architecture of 2 of our lab
     bases (hundreds to thousands each). This replaces the random mixed
     roster, which gave each query only 0–252 same-shape rivals.
   - Track A writes the launch card by Nov 12; the overseer launches it.
4. **Check the reversed SFT order (track C, by Nov 11).**
   - Likely cause: for base→SFT edges, the witness was the SFT's own DPO
     child. A descendant of the child sits closer to the child, so the rule
     "the parent is the one closer to the witness" flips.
   - Re-score with witnesses that descend from neither model (sibling
     fine-tunes of the same base), pass rule first.
   - If that fixes it, the order signal is real with a correct witness
     choice; if not, report it as a real limit.
5. **Steward rules (D10):**
   - plain language for everything the overseer reads;
   - no track idle for more than a working day while plan items are open;
   - "cannot verify" must come with a redesign, not a closure;
   - the necessity rule chooses the most decisive next test; it is not a
     reason to stop.

## Why each item is necessary (D1.6)

1. **Real cases:** the paper's headline (exact marks beat published methods)
   means nothing outside our lab without them, and existing data has none.
2. **Heretic:** the practical safety use depends on detecting the tool people
   actually use. The 4 models exist already, so the test is cheap.
3. **Scaling:** claim C1's "at scale", and the field's main open question
   (accuracy as the number of rivals grows), need many same-shape relatives.
   Existing data has at most 252 per query.
4. **Witness check:** cheap, uses existing data, and decides whether an order
   signal for ordinary fine-tunes exists.
5. **Steward rules:** the overseer cannot steer what they cannot read, and
   idle tracks miss Dec 12.
